Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  SeshScore by GV Tech

No data collection

SeshScore does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

No account required No analytics No ads Works offline

What is stored on your device

The following data is saved locally on your device only and never leaves it:

  • Player names and scores you enter
  • Game history (up to 30 sessions)
  • Your app preferences (sort order, game mode, round settings)

This data is stored in your device's local storage. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Fonts

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.google.com). This is a standard CDN request and subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
