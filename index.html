How it works

Up and running
in 30 seconds

1
Choose your game
Pick from 9 built-in profiles: Uno, Darts, Hearts, Catan and more, or go fully custom. Win target, rounds and scoring all set themselves.
2
Add your players
Type a name and tap +. Reorder with arrows. Clear all and start fresh whenever you like.
3
Tap to score
Hit +/− or enter a custom value. Rounds advance automatically. Win detection fires a full scoreboard popup the moment it's over.
Round tracking

Never lose count
of the round again.

SeshScore tracks rounds automatically. The moment every player has scored or passed, the round advances with a toast notification.

Auto-advance: round bumps when all players score or pass
Pass (0 pts): mark a turn without changing score
Max rounds: cap the game at X rounds, winner announced at the end
Undo restores round: undoing a score walks the counter back too
